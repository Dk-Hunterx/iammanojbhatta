# DK Hunter Daily Life Toolkit

**106+ free tools for everyday life** — money, study, health, dates, photos, documents, text and fun. No sign-up, no uploads: everything runs in your browser, on your own device.

**Use it online:** https://dk-hunterx.github.io/iammanojbhatta/daily-life-toolkit/

Customized and maintained by [DK Hunter](https://github.com/Dk-Hunterx) — free for everyone.

## Tool categories

- **Money & Finance:** EMI, interest, FD, VAT, salary, savings, expense tracking and more.
- **Study & Teaching:** NEB GPA, attendance, exam marks planner, flashcards, study timer and typing test.
- **Date & Time:** Age, Nepali date conversion, world clock, countdown and working days.
- **Health & Body:** BMI, BMR, water intake, sleep and body-fat estimators.
- **Images & Documents:** Image resizing/compression, image-to-PDF, PDF merge and document conversion.
- **Math & Converters:** Calculator, percentages, geometry, units, area including Ropani and Bigha.
- **Developer & Text:** JSON formatter, Base64, URL encoder, hash generator, password tools and text utilities.
- **Fun & Daily:** Dice, coin flip, spin wheel, shopping list and habit tracker.

All tools are designed to run locally in your browser. Check each tool's own page for its exact behavior and any external library requirements.
