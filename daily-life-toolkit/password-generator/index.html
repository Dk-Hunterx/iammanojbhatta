Stay Safe Online

Password Generator

Strong, random passwords made on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere.

Why this is safe

Passwords are created with your browser's cryptographic random generator (the same strength banks use), right on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved. Use a different password for every account, and keep them in a password manager.

Built by Manoj Bhatta · Free tool, works offline, your data stays in your browser.
